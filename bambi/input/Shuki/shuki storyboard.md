
# AI Storyboard Production Guide

## Global Style (append to every image prompt)
**illustrative realism, 90% realism, Disney Pixar for adults, cinematic lighting, warm golden color palette, emotional storytelling, ultra detailed, volumetric sunlight, soft atmosphere, realistic textures, premium animated film, subtle magic realism, shallow depth of field, 8K, masterpiece**

## Character Consistency
Use the exact same farmer across every scene. Israeli farmer in his early sixties, humble, warm smile, weathered face, practical work clothes, boots.

## Image Prompt
