# Nirel & Amnon — Wedding AI Film
## Master Scene Plan • ~8 minutes • Barsa's Point of View

> **Concept:** The entire wedding film is narrated by Barsa, the fluffy cream-colored female cat who believes she is the true protagonist, director and executive producer of Nirel & Amnon's love story.
>
> **Tone:** Cinematic wedding movie + mockumentary + romantic comedy + fantasy + occasional action/spy/sports-film parodies.
>
> **Visual rule:** Photorealistic cinematic animals, premium animated-feature quality, expressive but believable faces, elegant production design, warm golden wedding palette, sophisticated camera movement. Humans should appear only where specifically required; Barsa and Betta should remain visually consistent throughout.
>
> **Target duration:** ~8:00
>
> **Clip rule:** Every generated video clip should be approximately 5–15 seconds. Some scenes below intentionally contain 2–3 clips.

---

# Character Bible

### Barsa
- Female cat.
- Fluffy, adorable cream-colored coat.
- Elegant, expressive eyes.
- Intelligent, confident, slightly sarcastic.
- Never overly cartoonish.
- She narrates as if she is presenting a serious documentary.
- She believes the humans are lovable but fundamentally incompetent.
- She especially enjoys pretending that she organized the wedding.

### Betta
- Female Golden Doodle.
- Sweet, fluffy, slightly goofy and lovable.
- Nirel & Amnon's dog.
- Barsa's friend from Ofakim.
- "כלבת פריפריה מתוקה" — this can become a recurring affectionate joke, never mean-spirited.
- Visually she should feel like Barsa's warm, chaotic counterbalance.

### Nirel
- Creative, colorful, playful, artistic.
- Loved swimming and performing on stages as a child.
- Loves drawing, baking and cooking.
- Loves yellow, but likes all colors.
- Works in culture/creation/production/art facilitation with young adults on the autism spectrum.
- Loves beetroot kubbeh.
- Loves science documentaries, dinosaurs and geological layers.
- Funny, spontaneous, naturally weird.

### Amnon
- Football and geopolitics enthusiast.
- Loves blue.
- Knows world capitals.
- Loves Messi and Barcelona.
- Childhood "terrorist": messy, sweaty, poor diction.
- Loves rice with tanzia.
- Learned to make maklouba from Nirel.
- Works in marketing at a communications/broadcasting company.
- Humor is one of his defining traits.

### Couple Symbol
**V^**
- Their private shorthand for "אהבת חיי" / "ואתחה".
- It should gradually become a visual motif throughout the movie.
- Final appearance should feel emotional rather than like a graphic gimmick.

# GLOBAL VIDEO STYLE

Use the following principles across all video prompts:

- cinematic wedding film
- photorealistic fluffy animal fur
- expressive realistic animal animation
- premium animated-feature quality
- elegant production design
- warm golden cinematic lighting
- shallow depth of field
- volumetric light
- subtle lens bloom
- smooth physically believable motion
- dynamic camera movement
- strong visual storytelling
- no humans unless explicitly requested
- no subtitles
- no text generated inside the video
- no logos
- preserve character identity
- no random costume changes
- no extra animals unless requested
- avoid exaggerated cartoon physics except where deliberately specified
- comedy should come from timing, acting and camera language rather than slapstick distortion
