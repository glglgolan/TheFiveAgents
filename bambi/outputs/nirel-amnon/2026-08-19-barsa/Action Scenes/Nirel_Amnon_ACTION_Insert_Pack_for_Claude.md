# Nirel & Amnon — ACTION INSERT PACK
## Master Prompt for Claude • Additional transition scenes only

> **Purpose:** Create an additional pack of short, high-energy AI transition shots that can be inserted between the existing 56 scenes of the Nirel & Amnon wedding film.
>
> **CRITICAL:** Do **not** rewrite, reorder, replace, shorten, or otherwise modify the existing 56-scene storyboard. These are **additional inserts only**. The original scenes remain the backbone of the film.
>
> Target additional material: **~50–65 seconds total**, distributed across the film. Each insert should normally be **4–8 seconds**. The inserts are designed to raise energy, create cinematic transitions, add comedy, and prevent the 8-minute film from feeling visually calm or repetitive.

---

# 1. CREATIVE DIRECTION

The film is narrated by **Barsa**, Nirel and Amnon's fluffy cream-colored female cat.

The visual language should alternate between:

- beautiful cinematic wedding-film imagery
- warm emotional storytelling
- absurd animal comedy
- sudden bursts of action
- fast camera movement
- aerial dives
- POV/action-camera shots
- objects flying through frame
- whip pans
- speed ramps
- dramatic reveals
- occasional impossible-but-believable camera movement

The goal is **not** to make the whole film an action movie.

The goal is to create a rhythm:

**calm → build-up → sudden action → comedy → calm → emotional → action → calm**

This contrast makes the action feel much more exciting.

# 3. HOW TO USE FIRST FRAMES

For every insert, explicitly classify the opening image as one of:

### NEW
Create a new dedicated first-frame image.

Use this when:
- the camera starts from an unusual angle
- the shot requires extreme perspective
- the composition is designed specifically for rapid motion
- the existing storyboard image cannot logically support the requested movement

### EXISTING
Reuse the opening image already created for the referenced main scene.

Use this when:
- the existing composition already gives a natural starting point
- the action can begin directly from that frame
- reusing the image improves character continuity

### HYBRID
Use the existing character/reference image as the identity source, but create a **new composition** specifically for the action shot.

This is often the preferred method for Barsa and Betta.

# 4. GLOBAL VIDEO PROMPT RULES

Every video prompt generated by Claude must be:

- cinematic
- highly dynamic
- physically coherent
- packed with visible action
- specific about camera movement
- specific about subject movement
- specific about foreground/background movement
- specific about transitions
- suitable for Kling / Seedance / Google Veo-style generation
- 16:9
- premium cinematic quality
- realistic fur and believable animal motion
- no humans unless the insert specifically requires them
- no random animals
- no random text
- no subtitles
- no logos
- no unexplained costume changes

### Camera language to actively use

Use terms such as:

- rapid dolly
- drone dive
- FPV drone
- low-angle tracking shot
- high-speed camera chase
- whip pan
- 360-degree orbit
- crash zoom
- speed ramp
- rack focus
- extreme close-up
- low-to-the-ground POV
- action-camera POV
- camera roll
- parallax
- foreground wipe
- object passing directly in front of lens
- seamless match cut
- camera acceleration
- motion blur
- dramatic deceleration

Do not simply write "the camera moves quickly."

Describe **how** it moves, **where** it moves, and **what motivates the movement**.

# 8. EDITING RULE

Claude should treat these as **transition shots, not additional story scenes**.

Do not give each insert a long narration paragraph.

In most cases, the existing Barsa voiceover should continue underneath the insert.

The action should visually illustrate or amplify what Barsa is saying.

### Example

If Barsa says:

> "ואז הם פשוט נפלו אחד על השנייה..."

Use A3.

The visual joke becomes:

**They literally fall through a roller coaster.**

---

# 9. IMPORTANT: DO NOT OVERUSE ACTION

The action inserts should be distributed strategically.

Avoid:

- two extreme action shots back-to-back
- constant camera spinning
- constant drone shots
- action during emotional dialogue
- action during the most important romantic moments
- turning every transition into a VFX sequence

The most important principle:

> **The action should make the calm scenes feel calmer, and the emotional scenes feel more emotional.**
