# סטוריבורד: Healing System — Hero Loop: The Thread of Light

> חוט אור זהוב יחיד עובר בשקט מפנים לפנים — נקשר לרגע לקשר של מתח ליד החזה, ומשתחרר בנשיפה — ונגמר ברוגע שמתמוסס חזרה לפריים הראשון.

**פרויקט:** healing-hero · **יחס תצוגה:** 16:9 · **שפה:** he

## לוק ויזואלי משותף

- **פלטה:** warm amber, honey gold and burnished copper highlights against deep umber / near-black shadow; wardrobe in muted earth tones (sand, oatmeal, sage-grey, olive, terracotta); skin tones rendered warm and natural, never orange
- **תאורה:** dark warm interior lit by a single narrow vertical slit of golden-hour light placed just to the right of each face (inside the central third of frame); hard sliver of light on cheekbone and eye, everything else falls into soft deep shadow; the golden thread is a secondary, self-luminous light source with a faint bloom
- **מראה פילם:** intimate cinematic portrait, 85mm lens look, shallow depth of field (f/1.8), soft out-of-focus glass panes and reflections in foreground, gentle halation around the light slit, fine 35mm film grain, slightly lifted blacks, no digital sharpness
- **תנועה:** very slow push-ins and lateral drifts only; subjects nearly still (breath, a slow blink, a gaze shift); the thread drifts like silk in still air, always moving left-to-right; no fast motion, no camera shake, no sparks or particle bursts
- **אווירה:** introspective, calm, safe, quietly hopeful — not hype, not mystical-cheesy; a feeling of being seen and of something finally letting go

### Kling settings

- **mode:** image-to-video from each scene's approved first frame (image_output)
- **model:** kling-video-v3_0 (or current Kling pro/master model)
- **duration:** 5s per shot, then trimmed (see each scene's trim)
- **resolution:** 1080p, 16:9
- **cfg_note:** keep prompt adherence moderate-high; one subject, one thread, slow motion
- **negative_prompt:** fast motion, camera shake, whip pan, zoom burst, talking, lip movement, open mouth, smiling broadly, sparks, fireworks, particle explosion, lightning, neon, multiple threads, thread snapping, extra hands, extra fingers, face morphing, identity change, flicker, text, watermark, logo

### Seamless loop

- **method:** Duplicate s1's head at the end of the timeline. Cross-dissolve the last 0.6s of s4 (timeline 13.4–14.0) into s1's first 0.6s, then set the export in-point at s1+0.6s and the out-point at the end of that dissolve, so the first and last frames of the file are identical. Total exported length stays 14.0s.
- **visual_match:** s4 ends with the thread almost fully absorbed into the light slit and a calm, nearly still face; s1 begins with the thread just appearing at the left edge — the dissolve reads as 'the thread returns'. Grade s4's last second and s1's first second to the same exposure/white balance.
- **grain:** Apply grain as one continuous loopable 14.0s overlay on top of the edit (not baked per clip) so the seam is invisible.
- **export:** 1920x1080 H.264 (high, ~8–10 Mbps) MP4 + VP9 WebM, no audio track; plus a 1080x1920 center-crop MP4 for mobile; poster frame = s1 frame at 0.0s.

### Edit notes

- **transitions:** s1→s2 and s2→s3: match-cut on the beat — the thread exits frame right of the outgoing shot and enters frame left of the incoming shot at the same height (eye-line, ~40% from top); optional 4-frame dissolve if the hard cut feels abrupt. s3→s4: 8-frame (0.33s) soft dissolve centered on the 10.0s beat (the 'release'). s4→s1: 0.6s loop dissolve (loop_plan).
- **speed:** All clips may be slowed to 85–90% (optical-flow / frame blending) if Kling motion feels too lively — calm wins over realism.
- **grade:** Unify all four shots: warm amber highlights, deep umber shadows, lifted blacks, gentle halation on the light slit and thread. Match skin warmth across the four very different skin tones — never push the darker skin tones muddy or the lighter ones orange.
- **safe_area:** Before locking, preview a 9:16 center crop: each face and the slit must remain inside it.

### Audio notes (planned — generated later)

- **status:** Planned only — generated later. No voice in this deliverable. Website plays the video muted; audio is an optional, separately-exported 14.0s loop (WAV + AAC) for an 'unmute' toggle or for social cut-downs.
- **music_pad:** Ambient healing pad, free-time (no rhythmic grid), a warm low drone in D with slow-swelling airy synth/string pad layers, soft low-passed texture, no melody hook, no bells-and-chimes cliché. Gently rises in density through s3 (subtle low-mid tension, a faint dissonant 2nd held over the knot) and resolves to an open consonant chord (D add9) at the 10.0s release, then thins out toward the loop seam. Must loop exactly at 14.0s — bounce with a tail-to-head crossfade so it is click-free.
- **heartbeat_sfx:** Soft, low-passed 'lub-dub' (felt more than heard, mostly <150 Hz), 60 BPM. Beat (lub) times: 0.0, 1.0, 2.0 | 3.0 (cut s1→s2), 4.0, 5.0 | 6.0 (cut s2→s3), 7.0, 8.0 (+2 dB, slightly fuller — knot tightest), 9.0 (back to normal level — knot starts to loosen) | 10.0 (cut s3→s4, softer, -2 dB, the 'release' beat), 11.35, 12.7 (slowing to ~45 BPM and fading, -4 to -6 dB) | 14.0 = 0.0 loop seam (beat returns at normal 60 BPM level, softened by the dissolve).
- **accents:** Optional, very quiet: a delicate glassy shimmer that follows the thread's movement (panned left-to-right with it); one long soft air swell / breath-like whoosh under the 10.0s release dissolve (non-vocal, no human breath sound required).
- **mix:** Pad around -24 LUFS short-term, heartbeat sitting just under it; no element should be startling on autoplay-with-sound. Fade nothing to silence at the seam — the loop must be continuous.

### Director decisions

- 4 shots / 14.0s total (3+3+4+4) — within the brief's 10–15s and 3–4 shots; overrides the skill's default 6–18 scenes because the brief is a short loop.
- Cast: 4 faces = 2 women + 2 men, ages 28 / 32 / 45 / 62, four different ethnic backgrounds; wardrobe kept in muted earth tones so no single person pops out of the palette.
- Daniel's look is derived from the inspiration contact sheet (curly salt-and-pepper hair, greying beard, linen shirt) as the 'style-defining' face — but described as an original person, not a copy.
- Screen direction locked: every face is in three-quarter profile facing camera-right toward the light slit; the thread always travels left-to-right and flows into the slit. This keeps the 180-degree line and makes each cut feel like the thread is 'passed on'.
- Knot placed on Kenji (young man), near his sternum under his open hand — a deliberate choice so the tension/release moment speaks to men too, not only women. Release is given to Amara (elder woman) for a grounded, safe ending.
- Kling reliability: the knot is NOT asked to form from nothing — s3's first frame already contains a loose tangle; the video only tightens it slightly and then unwinds it. Untying is easier to animate cleanly than tying.
- Mouths closed in every shot (silent loop; avoids 'talking' artifacts seen in the reference sheet where the man speaks).
- Cut rhythm locked to a 60 BPM heartbeat (1 beat = 1.000s): cuts on beats at 3.0, 6.0, 10.0; the heartbeat slows to ~45 BPM inside the release shot and the loop seam lands on a beat at 14.0 = 0.0.
- Seamless loop by cross-dissolve of s4's tail into s1's head (see loop_plan), plus a continuous grain overlay so grain does not 'jump' at the seam.
- Delivered muted (browsers only autoplay muted video); the planned audio bed ships as a separate optional file.
