# סטוריבורד: Terra Roast — SLOW MORNINGS

**פרויקט:** surreal-ads · **יחס תצוגה:** 9:16 · **שפה:** he

## לוק ויזואלי משותף

- **פלטה:** cream, warm beige, natural oak, soft off-white + brand accent terracotta and dark espresso brown
- **תאורה:** motivated natural window daylight from camera-left with physically accurate global illumination, soft contact shadows, real bounce light and specular highlights
- **מראה פילם:** shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with a Cooke S7/i 50mm and a Laowa probe macro for close-ups, true optical depth of field and lens breathing, micro-detail (surface pores, fibres, dust motes, fine scratches), accurate refraction and caustics in glass and liquid, subsurface scattering, real-world scale, natural film grain, ungraded-to-premium color
- **תנועה:** one continuous camera move: slow handheld push-in, accelerating orbit through the transformation, decelerating into a locked symmetrical hero frame
- **אווירה:** minimal luxury, calm → wonder → euphoric rush → elegant stillness
