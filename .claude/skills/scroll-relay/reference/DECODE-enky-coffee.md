# Decode: enkywebstudio coffee reel (instagram.com/reel/DeAP-bpsvy3, 10.0s, 1080×1920)

Analysed frame-by-frame on 2026-10-10 (contact sheets every 0.33s). The user's brief: "the continuity flowing downward, the moving elements, the machine opening into its parts, each time another part revealed — the creativity in every frame".

## What it actually is
**One locked camera shot** of a laptop in a sunlit room. ALL the motion happens *inside the screen*: a "scrollytelling" website scrolls **down**, and every section is an AI-generated product video scrubbed by the scroll. Nothing breaks out of the screen. The magic is in the *edit logic of the page*, not in the camera.

## The 7 rules that made it feel "continuous and creative in every frame"

1. **Relay of matter (the key one).** Every chapter's *output* is the next chapter's *input*: beans → grinder → grounds → dripper → coffee → liquid → cup. There is never a cut. The transition IS the object travelling across the section border.
2. **One direction: gravity, down.** Matter falls down while the page scrolls down. The eye is always pulled the same way, so the 10 seconds read as one gesture.
3. **Reveal cadence ≈ every 0.3–0.7s.** Something new enters every half second or less: a part, a word, a prop, a card. No frame is static for more than about 0.5s, yet the camera never moves.
4. **Exploded view = the "how it's made" beat.** The hero machine opens along its axis into floating parts, so you see the inside. It is the most memorable moment, and it doubles as a transition, because the stream falls out of the opened machine.
5. **Depth interleave of type.** Giant editorial words (GRIND, POUR, SIP, FIND YOUR CUP) are layout objects that sit behind and between the product parts. That gives a 3-D feel on a flat screen.
6. **Locked, real frame.** A static, warm, sunlit laptop shot grounds the fantasy. Realism comes from the room; creativity comes from the screen.
7. **Ends on the payoff.** The last beat is a usable UI (product cards, price, CTA), so the story ends in the shop.

## Compared with our MELT film
MELT has 4 big beats with *cuts and camera moves*, holds 1–2s per idea, and one break-out moment. This reel has **6+ micro-chapters in 10s with zero cuts**: continuity by matter and gravity instead of by camera. Both are strong. The missing ingredients the user felt in MELT were rules 1, 3 and 4.
